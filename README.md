# NoIcons
An Android launcher that removes app icons, helping to boost your productivity.

# Description
This app transforms your phone by adopting a minimalist layout designed to reduce distractions and the habit of compulsively checking apps. It makes finding your apps slightly more intentional and eliminates those attention-grabbing icons.
Apps are organized into a long, alphabetical list, positioned below the digital date and clock. To the right of each app, you will see a number indicating the count of pending notifications, based on data registered by the system.

# Permissions
Notification Access
The app uses device notifications solely to display the number of existing notifications to the user.
